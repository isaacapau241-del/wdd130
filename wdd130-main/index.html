Ghana

Ghana is a country in West Africa, bordered by Côte d'Ivoire to the west, Burkina Faso to the north, Togo to the east, and the Gulf of Guinea to the south. Its capital and largest city is Accra. Ghana was the first sub-Saharan African country to gain independence from colonial rule, in 1957, and it is known for its rich cultural heritage, vibrant music and art, and the friendliness of its people.
